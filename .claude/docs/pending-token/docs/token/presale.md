# Buying in the presale

The presale sells tokens at a fixed price, paid in SOL. Your SOL stays in the presale's own account until the sale is settled, and either you get your tokens or you get every lamport back.

It is a fair launch: every buyer in a phase pays that phase's price under the same limit per wallet, and there is no vesting. Everything you buy is yours in full once claims open.

## The two phases

A sale can run a **priority phase** and then a **public phase**. Each has its own price and its own most a wallet may hold from the sale, and the sale page shows both before you buy.

- **The priority phase** is for the wallets its rule admits, such as holders of a Lucky Ducks NFT. Sign in and the page shows your allocation, the most you may buy there. Your purchase carries a pass the platform signs for your wallet, so the allocation cannot be used by anyone else.
- **The public phase** is open to every wallet, unless the sale asks for the same pass there too.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/token/app-presale-allocation-banner.png" alt="The priority phase after signing in: the wallet's allocation and what it has bought so far"><figcaption><p>Your allocation is shown before you buy, never after.</p></figcaption></figure>

## Buying

Buying, claiming and taking a refund yourself need you signed in: connect your wallet and sign in with it. A connected wallet that has not signed in can read the sale and nothing more.

Every purchase is at least the sale's smallest purchase, except the last tokens left, which can always be bought. A purchase that would take you past the phase's limit, or past your allocation, is refused before you sign.

Your first purchase in a sale pays a small SOL deposit to keep your position open, the record of what you bought and paid. It comes back to you when the position closes.

Purchases can be paused. Refunds and claims never are.

## When the sale ends

What happens to your SOL depends on whether the sale sold its minimum share by the time it ended.

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="The minimum sold" %}
The SOL goes to the treasury, and your tokens are yours to claim once the platform opens claims, after the sale has ended. Everything you bought is released the moment claims open.

Claim whenever you like: one claim takes all of it and closes your position, and its deposit comes back with it. Claiming also pays for your token account for the token if you do not have one yet.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="It did not, or the sale was cancelled" %}
Every buyer is refunded in full: exactly the SOL they paid, from the moment the sale ends. The platform sends the refunds, and you can also take yours yourself from the sale's page. Your position closes with the refund, and its deposit comes back with it.
{% endtab %}
{% endtabs %}

{% hint style="success" %}
Your SOL can only ever go to the treasury after the minimum sold, or back to you. There is no third place it can go.
{% endhint %}

Your reserved tokens, at the foot of the token page, list every sale you bought in, with how far it has got while your tokens are still in it. Once your claim or your refund has gone through, its card shows the transaction, and a link opens it on the block explorer.

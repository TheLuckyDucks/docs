# Welcome

Lucky Ducks is a fully on-chain duck racing platform built on Solana. Every race is provably fair, every NFT is verifiable, and every prize is paid out by a smart contract. No house edge. Just ducks!

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/brand/docs-hero-race-canvas.png" alt="Five ducks racing across the pond with position markers above them"><figcaption><p>A five player race, mid-run. The animation is driven by the seed the oracle published on chain.</p></figcaption></figure>

## What you can do here

- **Race** in lobbies of up to 20 players, in WTA (Winner Takes All) or Podium Split mode.
- **Collect** four NFT collections that change how you race: Runners unlock advanced features, Boosts add speed, Tracks let you race on custom scenery, Cosmetics dress up your duck.
- **Compete** in scheduled tournaments where teams race for a single on-chain pot, split between the winning team's members.
- **Team up** with up to 20 players in a team. Your races feed its standing automatically, with no sign-up and no entry fee.
- **Stake** in SOL or any supported SPL token (Legacy and Token-2022). The platform takes a small treasury fee, the rest goes to winner(s).
- **Play fast** by pre-funding a vault and letting the platform sign your in-game actions, so joining a race takes one tap instead of a wallet approval.

## Direct contact

- **Email**; [contact@theluckyducks.com](mailto:contact@theluckyducks.com). Best for partnership inquiries, bug reports with sensitive details, and anything that doesn't fit a public channel.

Telegram-verified players can opt in to have their `@handle` tagged in race-win and tournament announcements (see [Player verification](../trust/verification.md#telegram-handle-in-group-announcements)). The bot that posts those announcements has its own page: [The Lucky Ducks Telegram bot](telegram-bot.md).

## What goes where

| Channel  | Best for                                                 |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Email    | Sensitive issues, partnerships, sponsorship, press       |
| Telegram | Quick questions, community chat, real-time announcements |
| X        | Public updates, release notes, marketing                 |
| YouTube  | Recaps, tutorials, feature deep-dives                    |
| TikTok   | Short highlight clips                                    |

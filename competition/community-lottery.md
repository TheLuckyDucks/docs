# The community lottery

On UK draw days a box appears asking you to pick one number. Everyone who picks contributes one number to a single shared line, and Lucky Ducks plays that line in the real draw.

You pick one number. The flock picks the rest.

## How it works

You need to be signed in, whether that is with a wallet or through a social login. The box does not appear for a guest.

A round opens during a set window on a day the UK actually draws. The first players to pick fill the line, one number each, in the order they tap.

| Game         | Draw days           | What the line needs                               |
| ------------ | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Set For Life | Monday, Thursday    | 5 numbers from 1 to 47, then 1 Life Ball (1-10)   |
| EuroMillions | Tuesday, Friday     | 5 numbers from 1 to 50, then 2 Lucky Stars (1-12) |
| Lotto        | Wednesday, Saturday | 6 numbers from 1 to 59                            |

Sunday has no round, because nothing is drawn on a Sunday.

The box shows the line as it fills, live. When someone takes a number it appears in the row for everyone watching, and when the last slot is filled the box closes itself.

## Picking a number

Numbers already taken are crossed out and cannot be chosen. **Tapping a free number takes it straight away.** There is no confirm step, and no way to change it afterwards, so pick the one you want.

**Which numbers you can choose depends on where you land in the line.** Once the main numbers are all taken, the next players are picking Lucky Stars or the Life Ball instead, and those come from a much smaller range. The box tells you which one you are choosing and shows only the numbers that are valid for it.

You get one number per round. Once yours is in, the box will not ask you again, on this device or any other.

## If your pick does not go through

Rounds fill fast, so a tap can arrive a moment too late. The box tells you which happened:

- **Someone just took that number.** Another player took it while you were deciding. Pick a different one.
- **The line filled.** Every slot went before your tap landed.
- **Picking has closed.** The window ended.
- **You have already picked.** You hold a slot in this round already.

Nothing is charged and nothing is lost in any of these. The lottery costs you nothing to take part in.

## If you would rather not

Choose **No thanks** and the box will not come back for that round. It will offer again on the next draw day.

## Following along in Telegram

Channels can opt in to lottery announcements: when a window opens, each number as it is taken, and the finished line. Ask a channel admin to switch it on, or see [The Lucky Ducks Telegram bot](../help/telegram-bot.md).

## Things worth knowing

The top prize shown in the box is the one the National Lottery is advertising for that draw. It moves between draws, and for Set For Life it is not a cash sum at all.

Picking a number is not a bet and does not buy you a ticket. It is a shared line the community chooses together.
